# Four patches measured as losses on 13 Nov - do not re-try without new evidence

Each of these applies cleanly to HEAD and each was measured. They are kept so the next person does
not spend a day rediscovering the number.

## `carrier-hairline.patch` - 8 pass, 1 regression

`POST_TABLE_TW` 80 -> 1 at `DocxWriter.java:4518`. The 4 pt is charged four times (`:2764`, `:4493`,
`:4502`, `:4511`). Through a rebuilt engine: 8 of 81 fail->pass, 0 regressions on those 81, but 1
regression on 87 currently-passing table documents (`gst-invoice-3line-items` 100 -> 75, with NEGATIVE
dy). Not clean. Direction conflict worth recording: with `sz=2` exact, Google returns ~23 % of the
asked height, so under Google the carrier is probably already ~3 pt SHORT while under Word it is 4 pt
too tall. The durable fix is the `w:before` + `w:line=1` form already established for the spacer,
extended to the carrier - that was not built.

## `fitter-google-budget.patch` - correct, and changes nothing

Makes `fitPage` charge Google's advance instead of Word's. 250 insertions, builds clean, 208/208
converted. It moves NO page boundary - `BREAK_WHEN_UNFITTABLE` is false (`:171`) so the only route to
`breakPage` is dead, measured `broke=0` on all 208, and every docx has exactly as many page segments as
its source. It flips `spendGaps` on 20 documents and the squeeze on 2, in a direction nobody has
measured on Google. 158 of 208 documents emit bit-identical layout under either budget. Keep it as a
prerequisite if the line box is ever fixed; it is not a fix.

## `18-median-dy-cell-row-height.patch` - net LOSS, 62 -> 73

Writes `w:trHeight` always and as `hRule="exact"` on a one-line row. Measured on LibreOffice: HEAD
fails 62 with median |med_dy| 1.15 pt, patched fails 73 with 1.45 pt. The regression is a uniform -3
to -5 pt on table documents. A second variant (cell box cut to 12 twips with the baseline in
`w:before`) failed 72. The mechanism it targets is real - 72.7 % of text paragraphs are inside
`w:tbl` and the cell box is a median 2.19 em, which Google reads 4.85 pt short per line against 0.98
for body - but neither arrangement of it measures better on the only ruler available.

## `constant-line-box-flowrow.patch` - 100 % coverage, visibly breaks cells

Extends the constant 12-twip box to `flowRow` and table cells: coverage 0 -> 100 % (233/233 and
450/450 lines). But in a table cell whose row height is already pinned, the baseline is the only thing
left to get wrong: on a rendered 12 pt ruled table, 0 of 20 cell spans cross a row rule unpatched and
20 of 20 do patched. 300_prescription_rx +1.48 -> +3.88 pt, 311_fir_police_form -0.39 -> +2.95. Its
only Google benefit exists above 12/k = 10.4 pt (Arial), and all three real documents are 7.5-8 pt.
Also recorded: `flowRow` is NOT the table path - it writes a tab-stop row and fires once in 683 lines
(0.15 %); the table writer is a separate site at `:4417`. And the earlier note's reason for skipping
cells is wrong: change only a cell's `(w:before, w:line)`, leave `w:trHeight` alone, and LibreOffice's
row pitch still moves (18.00 -> 17.50 pt). The row follows the cell.
