## The headline

The pipeline has reported "the Google after-side cannot be measured" since at least 24 Nov, and three
runs and one sub-agent read the 403 as a missing permission. It is not. The platform `upload` tool only
stages a file that is already **saved**, and `gverify.py --stage` writes under `/scratch/work/`, which
`upload` refuses before any Drive call - and that refusal surfaces as the Drive tool's 403.

    cp /scratch/work/p2w/out/<name>.docx /scratch/output/<probe>.docx
    upload(path=..., tool_slug="GOOGLEDRIVE_CREATE_FILE", toolkit="googledrive")   -> s3key
    GOOGLEDRIVE_CREATE_FILE(..., parents=["1OoSHtsv-..."])                        -> Google Doc id
    GOOGLEDRIVE_EXPORT_GOOGLE_WORKSPACE_FILE(fileId=..., mimeType="application/pdf")
    gverify.py --collect <name> /scratch/downloads/composio/<probe>.pdf

Run end to end on `31_times_body_arial_headings` it reproduced the stored verdict exactly (41/42,
`18_displacement`, med_dy 2.6, worst_dy 3.5). The two Google Docs the probes created were deleted, the
staged copies removed, and the ledger re-collected from the unmodified build.

## The class, and why it is 18_displacement

`classpick.py --leverage` says `18_displacement` is worth **53.1 clean documents**, the largest prize in
the corpus, and `classpick.py` itself refused a new class because 35 pending items come first. I took
the pending item that is a real blocker - the measurement wall - and then the class the leverage table
names, because the ledger says **24 documents fail exactly one check and 10 of those fail only
`18_displacement`**. Nothing in the corpus is closer to CLEAN.

The ten, all single-page, so the page-break construct cannot be their cause:
`16_right_aligned_amounts_txtpos` `303_discharge_summary` `31_times_body_arial_headings`
`320_balance_sheet_statutory` `325_demat_holdings_statement` `362_price_list_dense`
`36_embedded_subset_font` `402_calendar_month_grid` `403_crossword_puzzle`
`72_bank_statement_no_rules_right_amounts`

**Eight of the ten sit inside +/- 3 pt at the median** - the page is where the original drew it - and
they fail because the check convicts on the count of runs beyond tolerance, not on the median. On
`31_times`, paired run by run against the Google export: **mean dy +2.649 pt, sd 0.712, n = 29**, and it
does not scale with the type (8 pt -> 2.50, 12 pt -> 2.15). One page-level constant of about 2.6 pt, not
29 constructs. It fails the check by **0.09 pt** against a 3.5 pt tolerance.

## Why nothing was committed to the engine

Every engine change on the table needs a 213-document regression, and the honest reading of this run's
measurements is that the candidates were **wrong**, not unlanded: auto is worse in Google, the grid is
not the constant, and the hairline charge is 0.00-1.82 pt on the documents it was meant to explain.
Committing a change three measurements point away from would have been the expensive mistake the brief
warns about. Four findings are committed instead, each with its number and what would overturn it.

## Next run - the lead, in order

   three or four of the ten solo documents. If the constant is one number corpus-wide it is a
   page-origin term worth 8 documents; if it differs per document it is not one defect. The med_dy
   spread (-10.2 to +8.2) says it already differs, so expect the second.
2. **The `hRule="atLeast"` row family.** Nine of the ten carry it; the only two with a real magnitude
   (`320` 21 rows, `325` 12 rows) carry the most, and `325`'s worst run is 38.1 pt.
3. **Use the Google loop for every attempt from now on** - about 90 seconds per document, and it is the
   only measurement that counts. The copy step is in `gverify.py`'s docstring now.
4. `RUN.json` `attempts` was zeroed by a `--heartbeat` after a rebase and set back to 1 by hand, and the
   run window moved with it; both are restored to the true claim (10:54) and the bug is written up in
