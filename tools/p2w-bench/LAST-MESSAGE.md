### The mechanism, and what was measured in Google

A page's top gap is emitted as `w:spacing w:before` on the first paragraph of the sheet
(`DocxWriter.java:4753`, `:3823`). **Google Docs drops space-before at the top of a page**, so page 0 keeps
its gap and every continuation page lands high by it — which is why the check's median equals the page-first
gap, one sign, continuation pages only. `w:pgMar w:top` is honoured at a page top.

The change moves that gap from the flow into the section margin: `pgMar w:top = G`, every page-first
`w:before` reduced by `G`, floored at 0. `flowTw`, `budgetTw`, `resync` and `fitPage` are untouched, so page
breaking does not move — only the bytes do. It touches **197 of 213** documents.

Before = the recorded verdict at HEAD (engine `981ff1fa35e2cf67`). After = a fresh Google render of the
patched `.docx`, exported from Drive, scored by `gvbatch.py` on engine `771234e164611275`.
