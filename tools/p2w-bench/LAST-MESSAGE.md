## What I chose, and why

The rotation said `18_displacement`, worth **54.4** CLEAN documents by `classpick.py --leverage` - six
times the next check. I did not take it, and the reason is not that it is hard.

`18_displacement` needs an **engine** change. Every engine change moves the engine fingerprint, which
makes the commit gate demand a 213-document .docx re-measure that `p2w.py` cannot finish inside one pod
(`BLOCKERS.md`). The 26 Nov relay run hit exactly this wall: its annotation-meta txt patch was measured
CLEAN on 600 and could not be landed. Taking 18 first would have been the same wall a fifth time, and
the slot would have ended with a measurement nobody could commit.

So I took the one class where a change is **landable inside the slot and provable end to end**: the .txt
instrument. It touches no engine source, so `txt_gate.py --gate` can prove `benchmarks/RANKING.csv` did
not move - and it did not (`b2fce93d8aebf91f`).

## The two faults, measured before and after

**t02 `_ruler()` fitted its character width with a median, which a rounding artefact outvotes.**
On `334_patent_document` 22 of 32 pairs are `(x = 28.0, col = 5)` - the body margin, where
`round(28/5.1) = 5` inflates the ratio to 5.60 - and the other 10 are the real ruler at 5.00-5.13. The
median returns 5.600, the writer used 5.100, and the check charged the document 69 % for its own
estimator's error. Replaced with `mid = sum(x*col)/sum(col*col)`: a pair at column 60 is 144x the
evidence of one at column 5, which is the right weighting when the error is rounding of `x/mid`.
Accepted only when it is a plausible character width and within 2x of the median, so a degenerate pair
set cannot move the ruler into nonsense. **An estimator fix, not a tolerance change** - a .txt with
randomised indents still fails, the test that made the earlier "abstain on a bad fit" version
unacceptable on 19 Nov.

**t08 keyed on the bbox top, which is not a vertical position.** `39_small_caps_letter_spacing` draws its
title twice on one baseline: a 1 pt white invisible duplicate at `y0 = 65.11` and the 15 pt
letter-spaced one at `y0 = 52.64`. Keying on the bbox top invents a top-to-bottom relation between two
pieces of one visual line. Keyed on the baseline the same predicate reports 23 of 23.

## Obstacles cleared

The pod arrived with the commit gate **hung**, not slow. `install-gate.sh` installs the pre-commit hook
and then runs `pod-prep.sh`, which converts the corpus; the call was killed at 300 s leaving an
`index.lock`, so every later `git commit` re-entered a 213-document build and timed out - including the
claim push itself. Cleared by running `corpus-slice.py` twice (64 to go, then DONE at 213 of 213, stamp
written for engine `981ff1fa35e2cf67`) and moving the stale lock aside; the gate then passed in seconds.
**The corpus build is a prerequisite of the claim, not of the measurement** - a cold pod cannot push its
own claim until it is done.
